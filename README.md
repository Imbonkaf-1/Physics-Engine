Its a physics engine that simulates the motion of a ball when its thrown/rolled off a height. 
It simulates forces like 
gravity, horizontal acceleration, horizontal velocity, terminal velocity, dragforce
(I added gravity and dragforce to my ball and terminal velocity just popped up as a result. Neat!)

The ball bounces back up after hitting the ground whilst also being in motion horizontally.
It also has air resistance pushing it back horizontally.
I will be playing around more and adding more things.
It creates a CSV file for all the time stamps etc as shown to better observe them in a graph or something.
